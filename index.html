About The Race

The Race Thru The Redwoods is a 10K trail run through Henry Cowell Redwoods State Park — one of the most scenic courses in the Santa Cruz Mountains. The route winds under ancient redwoods, through meadows, and alongside the San Lorenzo River, with a moderate 300-foot climb midway through. We are in our 56th year of operation, presented by the Felton Business & Community Association. Proceeds support community events and local charities in Felton.
